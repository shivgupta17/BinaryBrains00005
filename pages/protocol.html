🩹 Minor Wound Care Protocol
Source: MoHFW / ASHA Guidelines · Revision 2024
RAG-Retrieved
1
Screen for immediate escalation criteria.
Deep laceration, arterial bleeding, pus, extreme redness beyond wound margins → escalate to RED immediately.
2
Clean hands thoroughly with soap or sanitiser. Wear gloves if available.
3
Irrigate wound with clean running water or saline for 2–3 minutes.
Do not apply raw iodine directly. Remove visible debris gently.
4
Apply appropriate sterile dressing.
Dry dressing for clean wounds · Non-adherent for moist wounds.
5
Monitor for warning signs every 30 minutes.
Increased pain, swelling, purulent discharge, fever spike → re-escalate.
6
Record treatment in patient encounter log with time and steps followed.
🌡️ Fever Management Protocol
RAG-Retrieved
1
Ensure adequate hydration — ORS or clean water (2–3 L/day).
2
Tepid sponging for temperature above 102°F.
3
Do not administer antipyretics without doctor approval.
Patient is on Amlodipine — medication interaction check required via Medicine Gate.
4
Escalate immediately if temp exceeds 104°F or patient has seizure / altered consciousness.
