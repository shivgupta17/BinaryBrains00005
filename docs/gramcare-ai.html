📋 Protocol-Based First-Aid Assistant

Steps retrieved from a clinician-reviewed protocol library — not generated by the AI from memory.

Minor Wound Care Protocol
Source: MoHFW Approved ASHA Guidelines · Rev. 2024
Approved
1
Check for conditions requiring immediate escalation.
Deep laceration, arterial bleeding, signs of infection (pus, extreme redness, swelling beyond wound margins) → escalate immediately to RED.
2
Clean hands thoroughly before handling the wound.
Use soap and water or sanitiser. Wear gloves if available.
3
Irrigate wound with clean running water or saline for 2–3 minutes.
Do not use iodine directly on wound. Remove visible debris gently.
4
Apply appropriate sterile dressing.
Dry dressing for clean wounds. Non-adherent dressing for moist wounds.
5
Monitor for warning signs every 30 minutes.
Increased pain, swelling, discharge, or fever spike → escalate.
6
Record treatment in patient encounter log.
Include time, steps followed, and patient response.
🌡️ Fever Management Protocol
Approved
1
Ensure adequate hydration — ORS or clean water.
2
Tepid sponging for temperature above 102°F.
3
Do not administer antipyretics without doctor approval.
Patient is on Amlodipine — interaction check required.
4
Escalate immediately if temperature exceeds 104°F or patient has seizure/altered consciousness.
