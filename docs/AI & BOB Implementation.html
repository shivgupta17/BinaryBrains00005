GramCare AI
AI-powered Virtual Clinic for Rural Healthcare
One-line pitch:
The most important design choice is that AI assists; the doctor decides. That is also much
closer to India's telemedicine framework: the official guidelines explicitly support a health
worker facilitating a teleconsultation with a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP),
gathering history/examination findings, and reinforcing the RMP's advice.
1. The core idea
Don't build:
Patient → Chatbot → Diagnosis → Medicine
Build:
Patient → Health Worker → AI Clinical Assistant → Safety/Triage Engine → Doctor when
needed → Approved Care → Digital Record
The AI's job is primarily to understand, organize, translate, detect risk, retrieve approved
protocols, and coordinate care.
GramCare AI turns a rural health centre into an intelligent virtual clinic by converting
multilingual patient conversations, vitals, medical records, and images into a structured
clinical case, detecting urgent risk, guiding protocol-based first aid, and connecting the
patient with a qualified doctor when medical decisions are required.
